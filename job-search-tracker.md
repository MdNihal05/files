# Job Search Tracker

Target: Product/startup companies, 1.5 YOE backend/full-stack. Last updated: 2026-09-02.

Strategy: **LinkedIn + Naukri for volume, Cutshort + Instahyre for signal,
Wellfound + direct company career pages for the companies you actually
want.** No single portal covers the market — don't rely on one.

---

## 1. Portals — setup checklist

Tier 1 (do these first, in this order):

- [ ] **[LinkedIn Jobs](https://linkedin.com/jobs)** — complete profile 100%, turn on "Open to Work" (recruiters-only visibility), set up saved-search alerts for target roles. Most funded startups post almost exclusively here.
- [ ] **[Naukri](https://naukri.com)** — highest raw callback volume, but expect recruiter/MLM spam once your number is listed. Filter by "Actively hiring" + posted-date, ignore cold calls unrelated to the role.
- [ ] **[Instahyre](https://instahyre.com)** — fill out the detailed profile fully; reverse-matching means recruiters only see you if you're a fit, so profile quality matters more than on Naukri. Best once profile is complete.
- [ ] **[Cutshort](https://cutshort.io)** — where Series A/B/C-funded Indian startups hire; connects closer to founders/CTOs than a generic ATS. Best fit for your target profile.
- [ ] **[Wellfound](https://wellfound.com)** (ex-AngelList) — smaller volume, real startup roles, salary/equity shown upfront.

Deprioritize: Indeed, Apna (better for volume/services hiring, not product-company SDE roles).
